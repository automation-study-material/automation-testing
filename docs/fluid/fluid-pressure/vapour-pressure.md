## Vapour Pressure — Effect of Cohesive Force and Temperature

### 1. What is Vapour Pressure?

**Vapour pressure** is the pressure exerted by the vapour of a liquid when the liquid and vapour are in **dynamic equilibrium** at a given temperature.

```text
Liquid molecules
      ↓
Some molecules escape from surface
      ↓
Vapour forms
      ↓
Vapour exerts pressure
      ↓
Vapour Pressure
```

### 2. Effect of Cohesive Force

**Cohesive force = attraction between molecules of the same substance.**

* Strong cohesive force → molecules are held more tightly.
* More energy is required for molecules to escape from the liquid surface.
* Therefore, **stronger cohesive force → lower vapour pressure**.

```text
Cohesive force ↑
       ↓
Molecules held tightly
       ↓
Evaporation ↓
       ↓
Vapour pressure ↓
```

### 3. Effect of Temperature on Liquid

When temperature increases:

* Molecular kinetic energy increases.
* More molecules can overcome cohesive forces.
* Evaporation increases.
* Therefore, **vapour pressure increases**.

```text
Temperature ↑
      ↓
Molecular energy ↑
      ↓
More molecules escape
      ↓
Vapour pressure ↑
```

### 4. Effect in Gases

For gases, **cohesive forces are much weaker** than in liquids because molecules are far apart.

When temperature increases:

* Molecular kinetic energy increases.
* Molecular speed increases.
* At constant volume, **gas pressure increases**.

For a fixed amount of gas at constant volume:

$$
P \propto T
$$

This is the **pressure-temperature relation (Gay-Lussac's law)**.

### 5. Important Comparison

| Factor         | Liquid                       | Gas                                |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Cohesive force | Relatively significant       | Very weak                          |
| Temperature ↑  | Vapour pressure ↑            | Pressure ↑ at constant volume      |
| Main reason    | More molecules escape liquid | Molecular kinetic energy increases |
